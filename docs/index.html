Paradox Town Uploader

Paradox Town Uploader is a private publishing tool run by Axon Learning. It uploads the animated history videos we make ourselves to our own YouTube channels: Paradox Town (Shorts) and Paradox Town Explained (long-form).

The tool runs inside our own build system after a video is rendered. It uploads the finished video, sets its title, description, tags, thumbnail and scheduled publish time, and does nothing else. It is not offered to the public, and it is only connected to channels owned by Axon Learning.

It uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See our privacy policy and terms of service.
